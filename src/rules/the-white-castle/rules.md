# The White Castle — Rules Reference

## Overview

The White Castle is a Euro-style strategy game set at Himeji Castle in feudal Japan. Players are leaders of minor clans competing for influence by placing dice, managing resources, and sending Clan members (Warriors, Gardeners, and Courtiers) into the castle grounds. The game lasts 3 rounds of 3 turns each, and the player with the most Clan Points wins.

**Player count:** 1–4 players
**Key mechanic:** Dice drafting — you pick dice from bridges and place them on the board or your personal domain to trigger actions.

---

## Key Concepts

### Resources
There are three resources, each tied to a Clan member type:
- **Iron** → used to place **Warriors** in Training Yards
- **Food** → used to place **Gardeners** in Gardens
- **Mother-of-Pearl** → used for **Courtier** Social Climbing

Resources are tracked on your Domain board (0–7 scale). You can never go above 7 or below 0.

### Coins
Coins are the game's universal currency. You gain or pay coins when placing dice (based on the difference between the die value and the space's printed value). Coins are also used to place Courtiers at the Castle Gates (costs 2 coins).

### Daimyo Seals
Special tokens representing the Regent's favour. Maximum of 5 on your Domain board. Used to:
- Advance past checkpoints on the Passage of Time track (costs 1/2/3 seals)
- Perform certain card actions
- Convert to resources: 2 Seals → 1 Resource, or 1 Seal → 1 coin (at any time)

### Clan Points
The victory currency. Track your score on the Clan Points track. Red fans advance your score immediately; golden fans only count at game end. If you pass 40 points, place a +40 tile; passing 40 again flips it to +80.

### Lantern Rewards
Every time you trigger a Lantern icon, you receive ALL visible rewards in your Lantern Area (the row of face-down cards on your Domain board). You build up your Lantern Area over the course of the game by sending Courtiers into the castle.

## Setup

### Board Setup
1. Place the main board in the centre
2. **For 2 players:** Remove all cards marked with ♦
3. Shuffle Steward (ochre) and Diplomat (indigo) decks separately, place 1 face-up in each castle room, keep remaining decks nearby
4. Draw 1 Daimyo card (grey deck) for the top floor
5. Place die tiles in castle rooms with dice icons face up — each room needs at least 2 different dice colours
6. Tiles placed in the Well go dice-side down
7. Set Round marker to space 1
8. Create a supply of coins, Daimyo Seals, and +40/+80 tiles

### Bridges and Dice
- Place 3 bridges in the river spaces
- Take 3/4/5 dice of each colour for 2/3/4 players
- Roll all dice and arrange on bridges in ascending order (lowest on left, highest on right)

### Gardens
- Shuffle Plant and Stone Garden decks separately
- Deal 1 Plant and 1 Stone Garden card below each bridge

### Training Yards
- Place 4 randomly selected Training Yard tiles in their spaces

### Player Setup
Each player gets:
- 1 Domain board
- 15 Clan members: 5 Courtiers, 5 Gardeners, 5 Warriors
- 3 Resource cubes (set to 0)
- 1 Fan token

Stack Influence markers randomly on the Heron by the Passage of Time track. Arrange Heron tokens on the Turn Order track in the same sequence.

### Starting Cards
- Deal starting Resource cards (orange, one more than player count)
- Place random Starting Action cards (yellow) below each
- In reverse turn order, each player picks a pair: Action card goes on Domain board, gain the Resource shown

## Playing a Turn

Each turn has these steps:

### 1. Pick a Die
Choose a die from either end of any bridge (never from the centre). If centre dice remain, the closest one slides over to fill the gap.

**Important:** Picking the die on the LEFT end of a bridge triggers your Lantern Reward.

### 2. Place the Die
Put the die on any space with a die icon — either on the main board or your Domain board. The die always covers an existing value.

**Coin exchange based on die value vs. space value:**
- Die is HIGHER → you GAIN coins equal to the difference
- Die is LOWER → you PAY coins equal to the difference
- Die is EQUAL → no coin exchange

**Stacking rule:** Only 1 die can be stacked on each main board space. In 1–2 player games, stacking is not allowed at all.

### 3. Perform the Action(s)
Execute the action(s) shown on the space where you placed the die.

---

## Board Areas

### The Castle (5 rooms)
- **Floor 1 — Steward rooms (3 rooms):** Actions are determined by the colour of the die you place. If a colour appears twice on the tiles, you get 2 actions. If a colour isn't shown at all, you cannot place that colour die there.
- **Floor 2 — Diplomat rooms (2 rooms):** Same colour-matching rules as Floor 1.

### Outside the Walls (2 spaces)
- Any die colour can be placed here
- Choose 1 of 2 available actions
- These spaces give access to the three main actions: Warrior, Gardener, and Courtier

### The Well
- Space value is always 1 (so higher dice earn you bonus coins)
- Unlimited dice can be placed here
- Gain 1 Daimyo Seal plus the benefits shown on the tiles
- A useful fallback when other spaces are blocked

### Personal Domains
Each die colour corresponds to a line on your Domain board:
- **Coral dice** → Courtier line
- **Black dice** → Gardener line
- **White dice** → Warrior line

When placing a die on your Domain board:
1. Gain any visible rewards (Resources, Daimyo Seals, coins, Lantern Rewards)
2. Perform the action shown on that line's Action card

---

## Sending Clan Members

The three main actions let you send your Clan members out to score points and gain abilities.

### Warriors → Training Yards
- Move your leftmost Warrior to any Training Yard
- **Cost:** Iron (amount depends on the Training Yard)
- Perform the actions shown on the Yard tile

**Scoring:** At game end, multiply the total value of your Warriors in Training Yards by the number of your Courtiers inside the Castle (not counting the Gate).

### Gardeners → Gardens
- Move your leftmost Gardener to a Garden card
- **Cost:** Food (shown on the card)
- Cannot place on a Garden that already has one of your Gardeners
- Perform the action shown on the card

**Scoring:** At game end, each Gardener scores the points shown on its Garden card.

### Courtiers → The Castle
Courtiers can do up to 2 actions (of different types) per turn:

**A. Request an Audience:** Place your leftmost Courtier at the Castle Gates. Costs 2 coins.

**B. Social Climbing:** Move one Courtier up 1 or 2 levels.
- 1 level = 2 Mother-of-Pearl
- 2 levels = 5 Mother-of-Pearl
- Must move the same Courtier (can't split between two)

**When a Courtier reaches Floor 1 or 2:**
- Flip your Domain board Action card face-down into your Lantern Area (strengthening future Lantern Rewards)
- Take the room's card and perform 1 of its light-background actions
- A replacement card is drawn from the deck

**When a Courtier reaches Floor 3 (Daimyo):**
- Gain a Lantern Reward
- Place Courtier in an available Daimyo favour position (if any remain)

**Scoring:** Courtiers score based on their final position:
| Location | Points |
|---|---|
| Gate | 1 |
| Floor 1 (Stewards) | 3 |
| Floor 2 (Diplomats) | 6 |
| Floor 3 (Daimyo) | 10 |

---

## Passage of Time Track

This track represents your clan's influence over the course of the year. Moving forward determines turn order for the next round.

There are 3 checkpoints dividing the track into 4 seasons. Passing a checkpoint costs Daimyo Seals:
- 1st checkpoint: 1 Seal
- 2nd checkpoint: 2 Seals
- 3rd checkpoint: 3 Seals

**End-of-game scoring by season reached:**
| Season | Clan Points |
|---|---|
| First (start) | 0 |
| Second | 3 |
| Third | 6 |
| Fourth | 10–15 (varies by space) |

---

## End of Round

A round ends when only 3 dice remain across all bridges.

1. **Turn Order Reset:** The player furthest along the Passage of Time track goes first next round. Ties are broken by whoever's token is on top.

**After Round 3, skip to End of Game.** Otherwise:

2. **Gardens:** If a bridge still has dice on it, players activate any Gardeners they have on that bridge's Garden cards (each Gardener's action fires once, in any order).

3. **Next Round Prep:**
   - Re-roll all dice by colour and place back on bridges in ascending order
   - Advance the Round marker

---

## End of Game Scoring

After the final round, add up these categories:

### Remaining Resources
- Every 5 coins = 1 Clan Point (rounded down)
- Every 5 Daimyo Seals = 1 Clan Point (rounded down)
- 3–6 units of any Resource = 1 Clan Point
- 7 units of any Resource = 2 Clan Points

### Passage of Time
Score based on the season you reached (see table above).

### Clan Members
- **Courtiers in Castle:** 1/3/6/10 points by floor (Gate/Floor 1/Floor 2/Floor 3)
- **Warriors in Training Yards:** Total Warrior value × number of Courtiers inside the Castle (not counting Gate)
- **Gardeners in Gardens:** Points shown on each Garden card

### Golden Fans
Move your Fan token up for any golden fans collected during the game — these are now scored.

**Tiebreaker:** Whoever is higher in turn order wins.

## Solitaire Mode: Tokugawa Clan's Visit

### Setup
Set up a 2-player game but randomly draw your starting cards instead of choosing. Pick a rival colour and set difficulty:

| | Easy | Medium | Hard |
|---|---|---|---|
| Starting Clan Points | 0 | 3 | 8 |
| Heron Position | 2nd | 1st | 1st |
| Passage of Time | 0 | 1 | 3 |

The rival has no Domain board. They accumulate coins and Clan pieces separately. Prepare the Solitaire card deck with Bridge side facing up.

### Key Rival Rules
- The rival never pays any costs
- Never activates placement actions
- Gains only coins (not Resources)
- Can place any die colour anywhere

### Rival's Turn
Reveal the top Solitaire card showing a die reference (colour + position). If that die exists on the indicated bridge position, the rival takes it. Otherwise, keep drawing until finding a valid die.

1. **Place the die** on the indicated space. Rival gains coins if the die value exceeds the base value. If the space is occupied (no stacking in 2-player), the die goes to the Well instead.
2. **Execute actions** from the last 2 revealed Solitaire cards (top to bottom, left to right). If an action is impossible, the rival gains Clan Points equal to the current round number per missed action.

### End of Round (Rival)
After the turn order reset, the rival converts coins to Clan Points:
- If first in turn order: every 3 coins → Clan Points equal to round number
- If second: every 5 coins → Clan Points equal to round number
- Leftover coins carry forward

For Gardens: each activated Gardener earns Clan Points equal to the round number.

Shuffle the Solitaire deck between rounds.

### Rival Final Scoring
The rival does NOT score remaining Resources or coins, but scores all other categories normally (Courtiers, Warriors, Gardeners, Passage of Time, golden fans).
