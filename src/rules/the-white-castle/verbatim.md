# The White Castle — Verbatim Rulebook

Source: Official rulebook by Sheepgames / Devir / Thames & Kosmos

---

## GAME SETUP

### Castle Setup
1. Place the main board accessibly for all players
2. For 2 players: Remove cards marked ♦, shuffle Steward (ochre) and Diplomat (indigo) decks separately, place 1 card face-up in each space, keep remaining decks on board
3. Draw 1 Daimyo card (grey deck) for the final level
4. Mix die tiles with dice icons face-up. Place one of each color in ♦ spaces, then fill remaining rooms randomly in numerical order. Each room needs at least 2 different dice colors. "The tiles that are placed in the Well must be placed with the dice side face down."
5. Position Round marker at space one
6. Create common reserve of coins, Daimyo Seals, and +40/+80 Clan Point tiles

### Bridges
7. Place Bridges in river spaces. Take 3/4/5 dice per color for 2/3/4 players. Roll and arrange on Bridges in ascending order (lowest left, highest right)

### Gardens
8. Shuffle Garden decks separately. Draw 1 Plant and 1 Stone Garden card per Bridge, place below. Return remaining cards

### Training Yards
9. Place 4 randomly selected Yard tiles in corresponding spaces

### Player Setup
10. Each player receives: 1 Domain board, 15 Clan members (5 Courtiers, 5 Gardeners, 5 Warriors), 3 Resource cubes at 0, and Fan token
11. Stack Influence markers on Heron by Passage of Time track randomly; arrange Heron tokens on Turn Order track in same sequence
12. Draw Starting Resource cards (orange deck, quantity = player count + 1). Place random Starting Action cards (yellow deck) below each
13. In reverse turn order, each player chooses one pair: Action card goes on Domain board; gain Resource shown and place in Lantern Area. Decree cards transfer directly to Lantern Area if granted

## PLAYING THE GAME

A game consists of 3 rounds. Each player takes 3 turns per round. Play proceeds by turn order; "once they have played 3 dice each and there are a total of 3 dice left on the Bridges," the round ends.

### Clan Points & Victory
Winner is determined by furthest advancement on Clan Points track. "When red-colored fans are collected, move the Fan token up the corresponding number of spaces." Golden fans count only at game end. Exceeding 40 points requires placing a +40 tile; passing 40 again flips it to +80.

### A Player's Turn

**Dice Selection Procedure:**
1. Choose die from either Bridge end (not center dice). If center dice remain, the closest die shifts to fill the vacant spot
2. Place die on main board or Domain board space with die icon. It always covers another value (printed or existing die)
   - Greater value: gain coin difference from reserve
   - Lesser value: pay coin difference to reserve
   - Equal value: no coin exchange
3. Choosing the left-end die grants the Lantern Reward from personal domain
4. Execute the space's action(s) in any preferred order

**Stacking Dice:**
Only single dice stack on main board spaces. In 1- or 2-player games, stacking is prohibited everywhere.

**Coins & Daimyo Seals:**
Black numbers indicate gains; red numbers indicate required payment. Domain boards show Resource tracks (0-7). Gaining moves cubes up; spending moves them down. Maximum of 7 resources per type; excess is lost. Resources cannot go below 0.

### The Castle
Dice may be placed in 5 rooms: 3 on Stewards' floor, 2 on Diplomats' floor. "The action or actions for these locations are determined by the color of the die that is placed there." On Stewards' floor, up to 2 actions can be performed if die color appears twice. "If there are no actions available for one of the die colors, dice of that color cannot be placed in that room."

### Outside the Walls
Two color-unrestricted spaces allow placement of any die color. Players perform "1 of the 2" indicated actions. These areas feature the three principal actions: Gardener, Courtier, and Warrior.

### The Well
Space value is permanently 1. Unlimited dice may be placed. Dice showing values above 1 grant bonus coins. "When a die is placed, 1 Daimyo Seal is gained along with the benefits indicated on the tiles there."

### Personal Domains
Each die color corresponds to a Domain board line and Clan member type. Coral dice → Courtier line; black dice → Gardener line; white dice → Warrior line. When placing:
1. Gain visible rewards (Resources, Daimyo Seals, coins, Lantern Rewards)
2. Perform action shown on corresponding card

### Sending Clan Members to Himeji Court

**Warrior:**
Move the leftmost Warrior to any Training Yard. "Placing a Warrior has a cost in Iron that depends on which Training Yard you place them in." Execute indicated actions in any order.

**Gardener:**
Move leftmost Gardener to a Garden card "that does not already have a Gardener from your Clan." Cost is in Food. Carry out indicated action.

**Courtier:**
Perform up to 2 distinct actions:

*Request an Audience:* Place leftmost Courtier at Castle Gates by paying 2 coins.

*Social Climbing:* Move one Courtier up 1 or 2 levels by paying 2 or 5 Mother-of-Pearl. "This movement cannot be divvied up among different Courtiers and must be done all at one time."

If ending on floors 1-2:
- Flip personal Domain action card face-down onto Lantern Area
- Take room card and execute one light-background action
- Replace room card from deck (if available, otherwise skip replacement)

If ending on floor 3 (Daimyo):
- Gain Lantern Reward
- Place Courtier in available Daimyo favor position (if available)

### Daimyo Seals
"Daimyo Seals represent vouchers from the clan of the Regent, which you can accumulate during the game." Maximum 5 in personal domain. Used to advance seasons on Passage of Time track or perform specific card actions. "At any moment during the game," exchange 2 Seals for 1 Resource or 1 Seal for 1 coin

### Lantern Rewards
"Every time you activate a Lantern icon, you receive the benefits of all the icons that are visible in the Lantern Area of your personal Domain, in the order you choose." Obtain additional cards through castle social climbing on floors 1-2.

### Passage of Time Track
Represents Clan influence. Moving forward on the track determines turn order next round. "There are 3 Checkpoints along the Passage of Time track that divide the year into the 4 seasons." Advancing past checkpoints requires paying 1, 2, or 3 Daimyo Seals respectively. Final game scoring depends on track position.

## END OF THE ROUND

When only 3 dice remain total on all Bridges, the round ends:

1. **Turn Order Reset:** The player with the furthest Influence marker goes first next round; ties broken by topmost token. "Arrange the Heron tokens accordingly."

After round 3, skip to End of Game without applying below steps:

2. **Gardens:** If a Bridge still has dice, activate Gardeners. "The players use each of the actions where they have Gardeners just once each, but in whatever order they prefer."

3. **Next Round Prep (rounds 1-2 only):**
   - Roll all dice by color and re-place on Bridges in ascending order
   - Advance Round marker one space

## END OF THE GAME

### Score Remaining Resources
- 5 coins/Daimyo Seals = 1 Clan Point (rounded down)
- 3-6 units of Resource = 1 Clan Point; 7 units = 2 Clan Points

### Score Passage of Time
- Second season: 3 Clan Points
- Third season: 6 Clan Points
- Fourth season: 10-15 Clan Points (as marked on token's space)

### Score Clan Members
**Castle:** Courtiers grant points by location:
- Gate: 1 point
- Floor 1 (Stewards): 3 points
- Floor 2 (Diplomats): 6 points
- Floor 3 (Daimyo): 10 points

**Training Yards:** Multiply Warrior total value by number of Courtiers inside Castle (excluding Gate)

**Gardens:** Each Gardener grants points shown on its card

"Whoever obtains the most Clan Points wins the game! If there is a tie, whoever is higher in the order of turns wins."

## SOLITAIRE MODE: TOKUGAWA CLAN'S VISIT

### Setup Changes
Establish 2-player game normally but don't choose initial cards—randomly draw them. Select rival color and place tokens per difficulty:

| | EASY | MEDIUM | HARD |
|---|---|---|---|
| Starting Clan Points | 0 | 3 | 8 |
| Heron Position | 2nd | 1st | 1st |
| Passage of Time | 0 | 1 | 3 |

Rival has no Domain board—just accumulate coins and Clan pieces separately. Prepare Solitaire deck with Bridge side facing up.

**Key Rival Rules:**
- Never pays costs
- Never activates placement actions
- Gains only coins (no Resources)
- Can place any die color anywhere

### Gameplay
On rival's turn, reveal Solitaire deck top card showing a die reference. "If there is a die on the Bridge of the indicated color in the position shown (left, center, or right), follow the steps described below." Otherwise, draw cards sequentially until finding a placeable die.

Once die is chosen:
1. **Move Die:** Rival gains coins if value exceeds base. With 2 players, no stacking—if space occupied, move die to Well instead, gain coins, ignore tile rewards
2. **Execute Actions:** "From top to bottom and left to right, carry out all the actions shown on the last 2 cards drawn and revealed" (or 1 card if first was used). "If it is not possible to carry out one or more of the indicated actions, your rival gains a number Clan Points equal to the current round number for each action they cannot take." Return drawn cards to deck bottom

### End of Round
Right after turn order reset, rival converts coins:
- First in turn order: every 3 coins → Clan Points equal to round number
- Second in turn order: every 5 coins → Clan Points equal to round number
- Excess coins carry forward

In Gardens step (rounds 1-2): Every successfully activated Gardener → Clan Points equal to round number

Shuffle Solitaire deck

### Final Scoring
Rival doesn't score remaining Resources/coins but scores all other categories normally

### Solitaire Icons Reference
- Garden icon: Place Gardener on lowest-point garden card not yet occupied
- Warrior icon: Place Warrior in Training Yard matching indicated cost
- Courtier icon: Place Courtier at Gate
- Movement icon: Move lowest-ranked Courtier 1-2 levels up; handle card replacement if reaching floors 1-2; place in empty Daimyo favor if reaching floor 3
- Points icon: Rival gains indicated Clan Points
- Influence icon: Advance rival's Influence marker indicated spaces
- Coins icon: Rival gains indicated coins
