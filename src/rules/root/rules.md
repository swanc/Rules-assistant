# Root: A Game of Woodland Might and Right — Comprehensive Rules Reference

## 1. Golden Rules

### 1.1 Rules Conflicts
- If a card conflicts with the Law, follow the card.
- If the Learning to Play guide conflicts with the Law, follow the Law.
- If you can follow both a general rule and a faction/hireling rule, follow both; if you cannot, follow the faction/hireling rule.
- The term "cannot" is absolute. It cannot be overridden unless explicitly instructed.
- Whenever it is unclear what order simultaneous effects should resolve in, or which player should make a decision, the player taking their turn chooses.

### 1.2 Public and Private Information
- Players may only show or reveal cards in their hand if explicitly instructed, but the number of cards in hand is public information.
- The discard pile can be searched and inspected at any time.

### 1.3 Negotiation and Deals
- Players may discuss the game and make agreements, but agreements are non-binding.
- Players may only give cards to each other if explicitly instructed.

### 1.4 Game Structure
- Each player's turn has three phases: Birdsong, Daylight, and Evening.
- "At start of" a phase happens before everything else in that phase.
- "At end of" a phase happens after everything else in that phase but before the next phase begins.
- After Evening, the next clockwise player begins their turn.
- You cannot interrupt an action, ability, or persistent effect with another effect unless it explicitly allows it (e.g., Armorers says "In battle...").

### 1.5 Pieces
- Pieces are limited by the contents of the game. Do not use proxy pieces if you run out.
- Each player owns the faction they choose in setup and the pieces listed on the back of its faction board (except items).
- The ownership of faction pieces cannot change (e.g., Marquise cannot use Field Hospitals on Riverfolk mercenaries or hirelings).
- If you are prompted to place, take, or remove pieces but cannot do so fully, you must place, take, or remove the maximum number possible. This does not let you avoid costs or prerequisites.
- If multiple pieces are removed simultaneously and that would trigger effects, remove all pieces before triggering effects.
- Some effects let you force a player or their pieces to act. Resolve this exactly as if that player were choosing to do this (e.g., if you force the Eyrie to move warriors, they benefit from Lords of the Forest).

## 2. Key Concepts

### 2.1 Cards
- You draw cards from the top of the shared deck and discard to a shared discard pile.
- If the deck is ever empty, shuffle the discard pile immediately to form a new deck.
- Each card has a suit: bird, fox, rabbit, or mouse.
- Most cards also have a craftable effect.

#### 2.1.1 Birds Are Wild
- You can treat any bird card as a card of another suit, even if you must spend, take, or give multiple cards of the same suit.
- **Forced Effects:** If you are prompted to discard or give cards of non-bird suits, you must treat your bird cards as the prompted suit.
- **Reverse Substitution:** If you are prompted to spend, discard, take, or give a bird card, you cannot substitute a card of another suit.

#### 2.1.2 Ambush Cards
- There are five ambush cards: one mouse, one rabbit, one fox, and two birds.
- You may spend an ambush card for its suit, but it does not have a crafted effect.
- You may also play one in battle to deal hits (see Battle section).

#### 2.1.3 Dominance Cards
- There are four dominance cards matching the four suits.
- You may spend a dominance card for its suit, but it cannot be crafted.
- You may also play one to permanently change your victory condition (see Dominance section).

### 2.2 Clearings and Paths
- The map of the Woodland is composed of clearings linked by paths.
- **Adjacency:** A clearing is adjacent to all other clearings linked to it by a path.
- **Suit:** Each clearing has a suit: mouse, rabbit, or fox.
- **Slots:** Each clearing has a number of building slots (white boxes). Whenever you place a building, it fills an open slot. You cannot place a building in a clearing with no open slots.
- **Ruins:** Slots marked with "R" begin the game filled with ruins. Ruins cannot be removed unless explicitly instructed (such as by the Vagabond's Explore action).

### 2.3 Rivers
- Many clearings are linked by rivers. Rivers are not paths, but can be treated as paths if explicitly instructed.
- Rivers do not divide clearings or forests (except on the Winter Map where the river does divide forests).

### 2.4 Forests
- Areas on the map enclosed by printed paths and clearings are called forests.
- **Adjacency:** A forest is adjacent to all clearings that touch it without crossing a printed path, and it is adjacent to all forests that are separated by only one printed path.

### 2.5 Rule (Control)
- A player rules a clearing if they have more total warriors and buildings in it than each other player.
- Tokens and pawns do NOT contribute to rule.
- If there is a tie between players in a clearing, no one rules it (exception: Eyrie's Lords of the Forest, Lizard Cult's Pilgrims).

## 3. Victory

### 3.1 How to Win
- The first player to reach 30 victory points immediately wins the game.
- If multiple players reach 30 or more victory points simultaneously, the player taking the current turn wins.

### 3.2 Scoring Victory Points
Each faction has a unique way to score victory points, but any faction can score as follows:
- **Removing Buildings and Tokens:** Whenever you remove an enemy's building or token, you score one victory point.
- **Crafting Items:** Whenever you craft an item, score the victory points listed on the card.

### 3.3 Dominance Cards
The deck has four dominance cards, which let you win without scoring 30 VP.

#### 3.3.1 Activating
- During your Daylight, if you have at least 10 victory points, you may activate a dominance card in your hand by placing it in your play area.
- Remove your score marker from the score track. You can no longer score victory points.
- **Mouse, Rabbit, or Fox Dominance:** You win immediately if you rule three clearings of the matching suit at the start of your Birdsong.
- **Bird Dominance:** You win immediately if you rule two clearings in opposite corners at the start of your Birdsong.

#### 3.3.2 Activated Cards
- An activated dominance card does not count against your hand limit and cannot be removed from play.
- You cannot replace an activated dominance card with a different one.

#### 3.3.3 Using Dominance Cards
- A dominance card can be spent for its suit.
- Anytime a dominance card would be placed in the discard pile, instead place it near the map to show it is available to be taken.

#### 3.3.4 Taking Available Cards
- During your Daylight, you can take an available dominance card into your hand by spending a card of matching suit.
- You cannot treat an available bird dominance card as a non-bird suit.

## 4. Key Actions

### 4.1 Craft
You can craft most cards from your hand to gain an immediate or persistent effect.

#### 4.1.1 Cost
- To craft a card, you must activate crafting pieces of the suits listed in the card's bottom-left corner.
- A crafting piece's suit matches its clearing. Each crafting piece may be activated only once per turn.
- A three-color question mark denotes a crafting piece of any suit.

#### 4.1.2 Immediate Effects
- When you craft an immediate effect (paper box), resolve its effect and then discard the card.
- If it shows an item, take the matching item from the item supply on the map and place it in the Crafted Items box on your faction board.
- If the card lists an item not in the item supply, the card cannot be crafted.

#### 4.1.3 Persistent Effects
- When you craft a persistent effect (stone box), place it in your play area. You may use its effect as described.

#### 4.1.4 No Duplicates
- You cannot craft a persistent effect if you have an identical one in your play area.

### 4.2 Move
- When you move, take any number (more than zero) of your warriors and pawns from one clearing and move them on a linking path to one adjacent clearing.
- **You Must Rule:** To take a move, you must rule the origin clearing, destination clearing, or both.
- **No Movement Limits:** A given piece can be moved any number of times per turn. If you are prompted to take multiple moves, you may move the same or separate groups of warriors.

### 4.3 Battle
When you battle, choose a clearing with any number of your warriors and pawns as the clearing of battle. You are the attacker. Choose an enemy in the clearing of battle to be the defender.

#### Step 1: Defender May Ambush (4.3.1)
- The defender may play one ambush card matching the clearing of battle.
- **Foil Ambush:** The attacker may also play a matching ambush card. If they do, the defender discards their ambush card and the ambush is cancelled.
- **Resolve Ambush:** If not foiled, the defender deals two hits immediately. Then the defender discards the ambush card. If no attacking warriors or pawns remain, end the battle immediately.

#### Step 2: Roll Dice (4.3.2)
- Roll both dice. The attacker will deal hits equal to the higher roll, and the defender will deal hits equal to the lower roll. If the rolls are equal, attacker and defender deal the same number of hits.
- **Maximum Rolled Hits:** The maximum hits you can deal from rolling equals the number of your warriors in the clearing of battle, whether you are attacker or defender.

#### Step 3: Use Effects (4.3.3)
- The attacker and defender may use optional effects (such as the Brutal Tactics card) that affect battle.
- If both want to use effects, the attacker chooses who uses them first.
- **Extra Hits:** Many effects deal extra hits, which are NOT limited by the number of warriors in the clearing of battle.
- **Defenseless:** If the defender has no warriors in the clearing of battle, the attacker deals an extra hit.

#### Step 4: Deal Hits (4.3.4)
- Each hit removes one piece of the other side from the clearing of battle.
- You score one victory point per enemy building or token removed.
- The side taking hits chooses the order in which their own pieces are removed, but all of their warriors there must be removed before any of their buildings or tokens can be removed.
- They also choose the order of any effects triggered by their pieces being removed.

## 5. Setup

### 5.1 Standard Setup
1. **Assign Factions and Starting Player.** Assign one faction to each player. Determine starting player and seating order randomly. Each player takes their faction board and pieces listed on its back.
2. **Place Score Markers.** Each player places their score marker on "0" on the score track.
3. **Draw Starting Hands.** If playing with two players, remove all four dominance cards from the deck. Shuffle the deck. Each player draws three cards.
4. **Place Ruins.** Place a ruin in each slot on the map marked with "R" (four total).
5. **Form Item Supply.** Place these items on matching spaces near the top of the map: 2 Boots, 2 Bags, 1 Coins, 1 Hammer, 2 Swords, 2 Tea, 2 Crossbows.
6. **Gather Other Pieces.** Place overview cards and two dice near the map.
7. **Set Up Factions.** In setup order (A, B, C, etc.), each player follows their faction's setup instructions.

### 5.2 Reach
To set up an interesting game, choose factions whose Reach values add up to at least the total reach recommended for your player count.

## 6. Marquise de Cat (Setup Order: A, Reach: 10)

### 6.1 Overview
The Marquise occupies the Woodland and wants to turn it into an industrial and military powerhouse. She scores victory points each time she places a building (sawmill, workshop, or recruiter). The more of the same building on the map, the more points she scores.

### 6.2 Faction Rules and Abilities
- **Crafting:** The Marquise crafts during Daylight by activating workshops.
- **The Keep:** The Marquise can place pieces in the clearing with the keep token, and other players cannot. Pieces may be moved into the keep clearing. If the keep is removed, there is no way to place it on the map again.
- **Field Hospitals:** Whenever any number of Marquise warriors are removed from a clearing, the Marquise may spend a card matching that clearing to place those warriors in the clearing with the keep token instead of the Marquise's supply. (Field Hospitals cannot be used on non-Marquise warriors such as mercenaries or hirelings.)

### 6.3 Faction Setup
1. **Gather Warriors and Wood.** Form supplies of 25 warriors and 8 wood tokens.
2. **Place Keep.** Place the keep token in any corner clearing. This is your starting clearing.
3. **Garrison.** Place a warrior in each clearing except the clearing diagonally opposite from the keep (11 warriors total).
4. **Place Starting Buildings.** Place 1 sawmill, 1 workshop, and 1 recruiter among the clearing with the keep token and any adjacent clearings, in any combination.
5. **Fill Building Tracks.** Place remaining 5 sawmills, 5 workshops, and 5 recruiters on matching Buildings tracks from right to left (leave leftmost space empty).

### 6.4 Birdsong
- Place wood tokens in each clearing with any number of sawmills, one wood per sawmill there.

### 6.5 Daylight
- First, you may activate workshops to craft cards from your hand.
- Then, you may take up to three actions, plus one action per bird card you spend (not as part of an action), in any order and number.

**Actions:**
- **Battle (6.5.1):** Initiate a battle.
- **March (6.5.2):** Take up to two moves.
- **Recruit (6.5.3):** Place one warrior at each recruiter. You may take this action only once per turn.
- **Build (6.5.4):** Place a building in a clearing you rule.
  - Choose a clearing you rule and choose a building type (sawmill, workshop, or recruiter). You will place the leftmost building of its type from your faction board.
  - **Pay Cost:** Find the building's cost at the top of its column. Remove wood tokens equal to the cost from the chosen clearing, any adjacent clearings you rule, or any clearings connected to the chosen clearing through any number of clearings you rule.
  - **Place Building and Score:** Place the building and score the VP listed on the uncovered space.
  - Building costs by position: 0, 1, 2, 3, 3, 4 wood.
  - VP scored by position: 0, 1, 2, 3, 3, 4 VP.
- **Overwork (6.5.5):** Spend a card matching the clearing of a sawmill to place a wood token there.

### 6.6 Evening
- Draw one card, plus one card per uncovered draw bonus.
- If you have more than five cards, discard down to five.

## 7. Eyrie Dynasties (Setup Order: B, Reach: 7)

### 7.1 Overview
The Eyrie wish to restore their once-dignified kind to former glory by retaking the Woodland. During Evening, they score VP based on the number of roosts on the map. They are bound by their Decree, an ever-increasing set of mandated actions. Each turn, they must take all actions on the Decree or fall into turmoil.

### 7.2 Faction Rules and Abilities
- **Crafting:** The Eyrie craft before resolving the Decree during Daylight by activating roosts.
- **Lords of the Forest:** The Eyrie rule a clearing when tied for most combined warriors and buildings there, as long as they have at least one Eyrie piece there.
- **Disdain for Trade:** Whenever the Eyrie craft an item, they ignore the listed VP and instead score only 1 VP. (They can still score extra crafting points from effects such as Master Engravers and the Legendary Forge.)

### 7.3 Faction Setup
1. **Gather Warriors.** Form a supply of 20 warriors.
2. **Place Roost and Starting Warriors.** Place 1 roost and 6 warriors in a corner clearing that is not the starting corner of another player and, if possible, is diagonally opposite from a starting corner clearing.
3. **Choose Leader.** Choose 1 of the 4 Eyrie leader cards and place it in your Leader Card slot. Gather remaining leaders face up near you.
4. **Tuck Viziers.** Tuck your 2 Loyal Vizier cards into the Decree columns listed on your leader.
5. **Fill Roosts Track.** Place 6 remaining roosts on Roosts track from right to left.

### 7.4 Birdsong
1. **Emergency Orders:** If you have no cards in your hand, draw one card.
2. **Add to the Decree:** You must add one or two cards to the Decree, but only one card added may be a bird card. You may play each card to any column, and each column can hold any number of cards.
3. **A New Roost:** If you have no roosts on the map, place a roost and three warriors in a clearing with the fewest warriors where all those pieces can be placed.

### 7.5 Daylight
1. **Craft.** You may activate roosts to craft cards from your hand.
2. **Resolve the Decree.** You must resolve the Decree starting with the leftmost column and moving right. In each column, you must resolve all of its cards (in any order). For each card, you must take the action listed for its column:
   - **Recruit:** Place a warrior in any clearing with a roost whose suit matches the card suit.
   - **Move:** Move from any clearing whose suit matches the card suit (you must move at least one warrior).
   - **Battle:** Initiate a battle in any clearing whose suit matches the card suit.
   - **Build:** Place a roost in any clearing you rule whose suit matches the card suit and which has no roost.
   - If you cannot fully take an action, you immediately fall into turmoil.

### 7.6 Evening
1. **Score Points:** Score the VP listed on the rightmost empty space of your Roosts track (0/1=0, 2=1, 3=2, 4=3, 5=4, 6=5, 7=5).
2. **Draw and Discard:** Draw one card, plus one per uncovered draw bonus. Discard down to five.

### 7.7 Turmoil
If you cannot fully take an action in the Decree for any reason, you fall into turmoil:
1. **Humiliate:** Lose one VP per bird card (including Loyal Viziers) on the Decree.
2. **Purge:** Discard all cards on the Decree except your Loyal Viziers.
3. **Depose:** Flip your current leader face down, choose a new leader from those face up, place on your faction board. Tuck Viziers into Decree spaces listed on new leader.
   - **A New Clutch:** If you must choose a new leader but none are face up, flip them all face up.
4. **Rest:** End Daylight and begin Evening.

### 7.8 Leaders Reference
- **Builder:** Loyal Viziers begin on Recruit and Move. Whenever you craft, you ignore Disdain for Trade.
- **Charismatic:** Loyal Viziers begin on Recruit and Battle. Whenever you take a Recruit action, you must place two warriors instead of one.
- **Commander:** Loyal Viziers begin on Move and Battle. In battle as attacker, you deal an extra hit.
- **Despot:** Loyal Viziers begin on Move and Build. Whenever you remove at least one enemy building or token in battle, you score one extra VP (two total per battle where you remove buildings/tokens).

## 8. Woodland Alliance (Setup Order: C, Reach: 3)

### 8.1 Overview
The Alliance works to gain sympathy of the Woodland creatures. Each time they place a sympathy token, they score VP. The more sympathy on the map, the more VP they score. Supporters fuel Revolts and Spreading Sympathy. Bases allow training officers, increasing military flexibility.

### 8.2 Faction Rules and Abilities
- **Crafting:** The Alliance crafts during Daylight by activating sympathy tokens.
- **Guerrilla War:** As defender in battle, the Alliance deals hits equal to the higher roll, and the attacker deals hits equal to the lower roll.
- **The Supporters Stack:** To take various actions, the Alliance spends supporters (cards on their Supporters stack). Supporters can only be spent for their suit and do not count against hand size. Supporters are face down but the Alliance may inspect them at any time.
  - **Capacity:** If the Alliance has no bases on the map, the Supporters stack can hold up to five cards. If a card would be gained but the stack cannot hold it, that card is discarded. If any bases are on the map, the Supporters stack can hold unlimited cards.
- **Removing Bases:** Whenever a base is removed, the Alliance must discard all supporters matching the printed suit of the base (including birds) and remove half of their officers (rounded up). If no bases remain and the Alliance has more than five supporters, they must discard down to five.
- **Sympathy Tokens:** The Alliance has 10 sympathy tokens.
  - **Placement Limits:** A clearing can hold only one sympathy token.
  - **Terms:** A sympathetic clearing has a sympathy token. An unsympathetic clearing does not.
- **Outrage:** Whenever another player removes a sympathy token or moves any warriors into a sympathetic clearing, they must add one card matching the affected clearing from their hand to the Supporters stack. If they have no matching cards (including no birds), they must show their hand to the Alliance, and the Alliance draws a card from the deck and adds it to the Supporters stack.

### 8.3 Faction Setup
1. **Gather Warriors.** Form a supply of 10 warriors.
2. **Place Bases.** Place 3 bases on the matching spaces in your Bases box.
3. **Fill Sympathy Track.** Place 10 sympathy tokens on your Sympathy track.
4. **Gain Supporters.** Draw 3 cards and place them face down on your Supporters stack.

### 8.4 Birdsong
1. **Revolt (any number of times):**
   - Choose a sympathetic clearing that matches a base on your faction board.
   - Spend two supporters matching the suit of the chosen clearing.
   - Remove all enemy pieces from the chosen clearing. Place the matching base there, and place warriors equal to the number of sympathetic clearings matching the base's printed suit. Place one warrior in the Officers box (this warrior is now an officer). Score one VP per token and building removed.
2. **Spread Sympathy (any number of times):**
   - Choose an unsympathetic clearing adjacent to a sympathetic clearing. If there are no sympathetic clearings, you may choose any clearing.
   - Spend supporters matching the suit of the chosen clearing. The number required is listed above the sympathy token on the track.
   - **Martial Law:** You must spend another matching supporter if the target clearing has at least 3 warriors of another player (including warriors they treat as their own for rule, such as mercenaries or hirelings).
   - Place a sympathy token in the chosen clearing. Score the VP on the uncovered space.
   - Sympathy VP track: 1st=0, 2nd=1, 3rd=1, 4th=2, 5th=2, 6th=3, 7th=3, 8th=4, 9th=4, 10th=5.

### 8.5 Daylight
You may take these actions in any order and number:
- **Craft (8.5.1):** Activate sympathy tokens to craft a card from your hand.
- **Mobilize (8.5.2):** Add a card from your hand to the Supporters stack.
- **Train (8.5.3):** Spend a card whose suit matches the printed suit of a base on the map to place a warrior in the Officers box (this warrior is now an officer).

### 8.6 Evening
1. **Military Operations:** You may take actions, up to your number of officers, in any order and number:
   - **Move:** Take one move.
   - **Battle:** Initiate a battle.
   - **Recruit:** Place a warrior in any clearing with a base.
   - **Organize:** Remove one Alliance warrior from an unsympathetic clearing, place a sympathy token there, and score the VP listed on the uncovered space.
2. **Draw and Discard:** Draw one card, plus one per uncovered draw bonus. Discard down to five.

## 9. Vagabond (Setup Order: D, Reach: 5/2)

### 9.1 Overview
The Vagabond plays all sides of the conflict while going on quests to increase his renown. He scores VP by improving relationships with other factions, removing warriors of Hostile factions, and completing quests. He must manage his pack of items, expanding by exploring ruins and aiding other factions.

### 9.2 Faction Rules and Abilities
- **Crafting (9.2.1):** The Vagabond exhausts Hammers to craft. All Hammers match the suit of his current clearing. If the Vagabond crafts an item, he may immediately take it face up.
- **Lone Wanderer (9.2.2):** The Vagabond pawn is not a warrior (cannot rule a clearing or stop another player from ruling one). The Vagabond pawn cannot be removed from the map.
  - **Full Removal:** Whenever an enemy player uses an effect that removes all enemy pieces from a clearing (Revolt, Favor cards, bombs, etc.) with the Vagabond present, the Vagabond damages three items instead.
- **Nimble (9.2.3):** The Vagabond can move regardless of who rules his origin or destination clearing.
- **Defenseless (9.2.4):** In battle, the Vagabond is defenseless if he has no undamaged Swords.
- **Items (9.2.5):** The Vagabond's capabilities depend on items. Instead of a Crafted Items box, he has a Satchel and item tracks. Items can be face up (ready) or face down (exhausted).
  - **Item Tracks:** When gained or flipped face up in the Satchel, Tea, Crossbow, and Bag items are placed face up on their matching tracks. When flipped face down, Tea/Crossbow/Bag on tracks are placed face down in the Satchel. Each track holds at most three items.
  - **The Satchel:** When gained, Boot, Sword, Coins, Torch, and Hammer items are placed face up in the Satchel.
- **Maximum Rolled Hits (9.2.6):** In battle, the Vagabond's maximum rolled hits equals his undamaged Swords (face up or face down) in his Satchel.
- **Taking Hits (9.2.7):** Whenever the Vagabond takes a hit, he must damage one undamaged item, moving it to his Damaged box. If no undamaged items remain, the Vagabond ignores remaining hits.
- **Dominance Cards and Coalitions (9.2.8):** The Vagabond cannot activate a dominance card for its normal victory condition. Instead, in games with four or more players, the Vagabond can activate a dominance card to form a coalition with another player, placing his score marker on that player's faction board. That player must have fewer VP than each other player except the Vagabond. If the coalitioned player wins, the Vagabond also wins.

### 9.2.9 Relationships
The faction board has a Relationships chart with four Allied track spaces and one Hostile box, holding a relationship marker for each non-Vagabond faction.

- **Improving Relationships:** You improve a relationship by taking the Aid action enough times in one turn. The number of Aid actions needed is listed between the current and next Allied spaces. When you advance, score the VP listed in the new space.
- **Allied Status:** If a relationship marker reaches the final space, you are Allied with that faction.
  - **Aiding Ally:** Each time you Aid an Allied faction, score 2 VP.
  - **Moving with Ally:** Whenever you move, you may also force warriors of one Allied faction to move from your origin to your destination.
  - **Attacking with Ally:** At the start of battle as attacker, you may treat warriors of one Allied faction in the clearing as your own. Maximum rolled hits = their warriors there + your undamaged Swords. You cannot use Allied warriors when attacking that same Allied faction.
  - **Taking Hits with Ally:** In battle where you treat Allied warriors as your own, you can take hits by removing them. However, if you remove more Allied warriors than you damage items in the same battle, that faction becomes Hostile at battle's end.
- **Hostile Status:** If you remove a warrior of a non-Hostile faction, move their marker to the Hostile box. Then check for Infamy scoring.
  - **Infamy:** Score one extra VP for each piece of a Hostile faction you remove in battle during your turn, except the warrior that made the faction Hostile.
  - **Moving into Hostile Clearings:** You must exhaust an extra Boot to move into a clearing with any warriors of any Hostile faction.
  - **Aiding Hostile Factions:** You cannot move a relationship marker out of the Hostile box by using Aid, but you can still Aid a Hostile faction to take crafted items.
  - **Coalition with Hostile Faction:** You can form a coalition with a Hostile faction. If you do, move their marker to the Indifferent space.

### 9.3 Faction Setup
1. **Choose Character.** Choose a character card and place it in your Character Card slot.
2. **Place Pawn.** Place your Vagabond pawn in any forest.
3. **Get Quests.** Shuffle your quest deck, draw 3 quest cards, and place them face up near you.
4. **Populate Ruins.** Take the 4 ruins from the map and the Boot, Bag, Hammer, and Sword items marked with "R." Place one beneath each ruin, shuffle each ruin stack, and return to ruin slots.
5. **Take Starting Items.** Take the items marked with "S" listed on your character card. Place Tea, Crossbow, and Bag items on matching tracks. Place all others in your Satchel. Return unused "S" items to the box.
6. **Set Relationships.** Take a relationship marker for each non-Vagabond faction and place on the Indifferent space.

### 9.4 Birdsong
1. **Refresh:** Flip two exhausted items face up for each Tea face up on the Refresh track at the start of your Birdsong. Then flip up three more exhausted items.
2. **Slip:** You may move into an adjacent clearing or forest without exhausting any Boots, even for moving into a Hostile clearing. This move ignores all effects that prevent movement out of a clearing (such as the Corvids' snare).

### 9.5 Daylight
You can exhaust items to take these actions in any order and number. If you exhaust a Crossbow, Tea, or Bag on a track, move it to the Satchel.

- **Move (9.5.1):** Exhaust one Boot to move, plus one Boot if the destination has any Hostile warriors. You cannot move into a forest. If in a forest, you may only move into an adjacent clearing.
- **Battle (9.5.2):** Exhaust one Sword to initiate a battle. Check your relationship with the defender.
- **Explore (9.5.3):** Exhaust one Torch to take one item under a ruin in your clearing, reveal it, and place it face up. Score 1 VP. If you remove the last item from a ruin, remove the ruin.
- **Aid (9.5.4):** Exhaust any one item, and give one of your cards matching your clearing to any player with faction pieces there (even Hostile). Then you may take one item from that player's Crafted Items box. Check your relationship with that player's faction.
- **Quest (9.5.5):** Choose a quest whose suit matches your clearing, and exhaust the two items listed on the quest to complete it. Place the quest in your play area, draw a new quest. You may score 1 VP per quest of matching suit you have completed (including this one), or draw 2 cards.
- **Strike (9.5.6):** Exhaust one Crossbow to remove an enemy warrior from your clearing. If that enemy has no warriors there, you may instead remove one of their other faction pieces. Check your relationship.
- **Repair (9.5.7):** Exhaust one Hammer to move one damaged item to the Satchel, keeping it on its current side (face up Tea/Crossbow/Bag immediately move to their tracks).
- **Craft (9.5.8):** Play a card from your hand, and exhaust the Hammers of listed suit to craft it. All Hammers match the suit of your clearing.
- **Special Action (9.5.9):** Exhaust one Torch to take the action listed on your character card.

### 9.6 Evening
1. **Rest:** If you are in a forest, move all items in your Damaged box to your Satchel or their matching tracks, and flip them face up.
2. **Draw Cards:** Draw one card, plus one per Coins face up on the matching track.
3. **Discard Cards:** Discard down to five cards.
4. **Check Item Capacity:** If you have more items total in your Satchel and Damaged box than your item limit (six plus two per Bag face up on matching track), remove items until you reach your limit. Removed items are removed from the game permanently.

### 9.7 Playing with Two Vagabonds
- Use both sets of "R" items and place two random "R" items in each ruin slot.
- Either Vagabond may complete any of the three face-up quests. Do not add extra quest cards during setup.
- When exploring a ruin with two items, that player may look at both and take one of their choice. A player cannot take an "R" item if they already have the same type of "R" item.

### Vagabond Characters Reference
- **Thief:** Start with Boot, Torch, Tea, Sword. Special Action: Steal — Exhaust Torch to take a random card from any player in your clearing.
- **Tinker:** Start with Boot, Torch, Bag, Hammer. Special Action: Day Labor — Exhaust Torch to take a card from the discard pile whose suit matches your clearing (you can always take a bird card).
- **Ranger:** Start with Boot, Torch, Crossbow, Sword. Special Action: Hideout — Exhaust Torch to repair three items, then immediately end Daylight and begin Evening.
- **Vagrant:** Start with Crossbow, Torch, Boot. Special Action: Instigate — Exhaust Torch to initiate a battle in your clearing. You choose attacker and defender, the order of building/token removal, and you remove pieces for each side. Score a VP per building/token of either player removed and per Hostile piece of either player removed.
- **Arbiter:** Start with Boot, Torch, Sword, Sword. Special Action: Protector — Before dice are rolled, a defender may enlist the Arbiter. The Arbiter scores 1 VP and adds all undamaged Swords to the defender's maximum rolled hits. Cannot enlist himself or be enlisted against himself.
- **Scoundrel:** Start with Boot, Boot, Torch, Crossbow. Special Action: Scorched Earth — Exhaust Torch and place it in your clearing. Remove all enemy pieces there. Pieces cannot be placed in or moved into that clearing with the Torch. Once you move out, you cannot move back in.
- **Adventurer:** Start with Boot, Torch, Hammer. Special Action: Improvise — Once per turn while taking the Quest action, you may treat one unexhausted item as any other item. When you exhaust it, also damage that item.
- **Ronin:** Start with Boot, Boot, Torch, Sword. Special Action: Swift Strike — Exhaust a Sword to deal an extra hit in battle (after rolling).
- **Harrier:** Start with Crossbow, Torch, Sword, Crossbow. Special Action: Glide — Exhaust Torch to move only your pawn to any clearing on the map, ignoring adjacency and paths, without exhausting any Boots.

## 10. Lizard Cult (Setup Order: E, Reach: 2)

### 10.1 Overview
The Lizard Cult seeks to overwhelm foes through sheer force of will. Where they rule, they build gardens. They score VP by completing rituals — spending cards to score based on gardens of matching suit. Their warriors gain acolytes through Revenge when they are removed defending in battle.

### 10.2 Faction Rules and Abilities
- **Crafting (10.2.1):** The Cult crafts during Evening by activating gardens whose printed suit matches the Outcast suit.
- **Hatred of Birds (10.2.2):** Bird cards are NOT wild for Cult rituals.
- **Revenge (10.2.3):** Whenever a Cult warrior is removed while defending in battle, it is placed in the Acolytes box instead of the Cult's supply.
- **Pilgrims (10.2.4):** The Cult rules any clearings where they have any gardens. This overrides the Eyrie's Lords of the Forest.
- **Fear of the Faithful (10.2.5):** Whenever a garden is removed, the Cult must discard a random card.
- **The Lost Souls Pile (10.2.6):** Whenever any card is spent or discarded (even a dominance card), place it in the Lost Souls pile instead of the discard pile. The Lost Souls pile can be inspected by any player at any time.

### 10.3 Faction Setup
1. **Gather Warriors.** Form a supply of 25 warriors.
2. **Place Warriors.** Place 4 warriors and 1 garden of matching printed suit in a corner clearing that is not the starting corner of another player and, if possible, diagonally opposite from one. Place 1 warrior in each adjacent clearing.
3. **Choose Outcast.** Place the outcast marker on any suit space in the Outcast box.
4. **Fill Gardens Tracks.** Place 14 remaining gardens on matching spaces of Gardens tracks from right to left.

### 10.4 Birdsong
1. **Adjust Outcast:** Look at the Lost Souls pile, ignoring birds. The suit with the most cards becomes the new Outcast. If that suit was already the Outcast, flip to Hated. If no one suit had the most cards, the marker stays on its current suit and, if not Hated, flips to its Hated side.
2. **Discard Lost Souls:** Discard all cards in the Lost Souls pile to the discard pile (dominance cards become available to take).
3. **Perform Conspiracies:** You can spend acolytes (return to supply) to perform conspiracies in clearings matching the Outcast, in any order and number. If the Outcast is Hated, conspiracies cost one fewer acolyte.
   - **Crusade (2 acolytes):** Battle in an Outcast clearing, or move (at least one warrior) from an Outcast clearing and then optionally battle in the destination.
   - **Convert (2 acolytes):** Replace an enemy warrior from an Outcast clearing with a Cult warrior.
   - **Sanctify (3 acolytes):** Replace an enemy building from an Outcast clearing with a garden of the Outcast suit.

### 10.5 Daylight
You may reveal any number of cards and perform one ritual per card revealed, in any order and number. Revealed cards cannot be used for any other purposes during Daylight.
- **Build:** In a clearing you rule matching the revealed card, place a matching garden.
- **Recruit:** In a clearing matching the revealed card, place a warrior.
- **Score:** Spend the same card you reveal (placing in Lost Souls) to score the VP listed above the rightmost empty Gardens space of the matching suit. You may perform this only once per turn per suit.
- **Sacrifice:** Place a warrior in the Acolytes box. You must reveal a bird card for this ritual.

### 10.6 Evening
1. **Return Revealed Cards:** Return all cards you revealed this turn to your hand.
2. **Craft:** Activate gardens whose printed suit matches the Outcast suit.
3. **Draw and Discard:** Draw one card, plus one per uncovered draw bonus. Discard down to five.

## 11. Riverfolk Company (Setup Order: F, Reach: 5)

### 11.1 Overview
The Riverfolk offer services to other factions while building trade posts along the river to score VP. They can also score through dividends from accumulated funds.

### 11.2 Faction Rules and Abilities
- **Crafting (11.2.1):** The Riverfolk craft during Daylight by committing funds to empty spaces on the Trade Posts tracks (no crafting pieces).
- **Swimmers (11.2.2):** The Riverfolk treat rivers as paths and may move along rivers regardless of who rules origin or destination clearing.
- **Public Hand (11.2.3):** The Riverfolk place their hand face up above their faction board. If another player takes a random card, cards are flipped down, shuffled, drawn from, then flipped back up.
- **Funds (11.2.4):** To take many actions, the Riverfolk commit and spend funds (warriors in their Funds box).
- **Trade Posts (11.2.5):** The Riverfolk score VP when they place trade posts.
  - **Trade Disruption:** Whenever a trade post is removed, the Riverfolk remove half of their funds (rounded up) and remove the trade post from the game permanently.

#### 11.2.6 Buying Services
At the start of another player's Birdsong, that player may buy Riverfolk services.
- **Cost:** The buyer places warriors from their supply into the Riverfolk's Payments box equal to the cost.
- **Number of Services:** Each turn, a player may buy one service plus one per clearing with a trade post and their faction pieces.
- **Vagabond Funds:** The Vagabond pays by exhausting items — for each item exhausted, the Riverfolk place one Riverfolk warrior in the Payments box.

#### 11.2.7 Riverfolk Services
- **Hand Card:** The buyer takes any card from the Riverfolk's hand. May buy multiple times if enough trade post access.
- **Riverboats:** The buyer treats rivers as paths until end of their turn.
- **Mercenaries:** During Daylight and Evening of this turn (except when battling the Riverfolk), the buyer treats Riverfolk warriors as their own for rule and for battle. The buyer cannot move them, count them toward dominance, or remove them except by taking hits. They remain Riverfolk faction pieces.
  - **Taking Hits:** The buyer must split hits — taking odd hits by removing their own warriors (if any), or their own buildings/tokens only if they have no warriors (including Riverfolk) in the clearing.
  - **Vagabond Mercenaries:** The Vagabond cannot buy Riverfolk mercenaries.

### 11.3 Faction Setup
1. **Gather Warriors.** Form a supply of 15 warriors.
2. **Place Warriors.** Place 4 warriors in any clearings touching the river.
3. **Fill Trade Posts Tracks.** Place 9 trade posts on matching spaces of your Trade Posts tracks.
4. **Gain Starting Funds.** Place 3 warriors in your Payments box.
5. **Set Starting Prices.** Place 1 service marker on any space of each of your Services tracks.

### 11.4 Birdsong
1. **Protectionism:** If the Payments box is empty, place two warriors in it.
2. **Score Dividends:** If there are any trade posts on the map, score 1 VP per two funds (warriors in Funds box only, not Payments or Committed).
3. **Gather Funds:** Move all warriors on your faction board to the Funds box.

### 11.5 Daylight
You can commit and spend funds to take actions in any order and number. When you commit a fund, move the warrior to the Committed box. When you spend a fund, return the warrior to its owner's supply.
- **Move (11.5.1):** Commit one fund to take a move.
- **Battle (11.5.2):** Commit one fund to initiate a battle.
- **Craft (11.5.3):** Commit funds to craft a card. Instead of moving warriors to Committed, place them on empty spaces of Trade Posts tracks matching the crafting cost suits.
  - **Export:** You may ignore the listed benefit, discarding the card, to place one Riverfolk warrior in the Payments box.
- **Draw (11.5.4):** Commit one fund to draw a card.
- **Recruit (11.5.5):** Spend one fund to place a warrior in any clearing with a river.
- **Establish Trade Post with Garrison (11.5.6):** Spend two funds to place a trade post and a warrior.
  - Choose any clearing without a trade post that is ruled by any player.
  - Spend two funds of the player who rules the chosen clearing.
  - Place the matching trade post and one warrior there. Score the VP listed on the uncovered space.

### 11.6 Evening
1. **Discard Cards:** Discard down to five cards.
2. **Set Costs:** You may move each service marker to any space on its track.

## 12. Underground Duchy (Setup Order: G, Reach: 8)

### 12.1 Overview
The Duchy wants to show Woodland creatures they would be better off as subjects. By swaying ministers to the cause, the Duchy scores VP and gains additional actions. But reversals of fortune lead to public humiliation and loss of ministers.

### 12.2 Faction Rules and Abilities
- **Crafting (12.2.1):** The Duchy crafts during Evening by activating citadels and markets.
- **The Burrow (12.2.2):** The Burrow is an unsuited clearing adjacent to each clearing with a tunnel token. Non-Duchy pieces cannot be placed in or moved into the Burrow. The Duchy always rules the Burrow (even with no pieces there).
- **The Price of Failure (12.2.3):** Whenever any number of Duchy buildings are removed, the Duchy returns their swayed minister card of highest rank (lord, then noble, then squire) to their Unswayed Ministers pile, removes its crown from the game permanently, then discards a random card. If multiple swayed ministers share the highest rank, the Duchy chooses which to return.
- **Tunnels (12.2.4):** The Duchy has three tunnel tokens. If prompted to place a tunnel but all three are on the map, the Duchy may first remove any tunnel from the map.

### 12.3 Faction Setup
1. **Gather Warriors and Tunnels.** Form supplies of 20 warriors and 3 tunnel tokens.
2. **Prepare the Burrow.** Place the Burrow board near the map.
3. **Surface.** Place 2 warriors and 1 tunnel in a corner clearing that is not the starting corner of another player and, if possible, diagonally opposite from one. Place 2 warriors in each adjacent clearing (except the Burrow).
4. **Fill Buildings Tracks.** Place 3 citadels and 3 markets on matching Buildings spaces.
5. **Collect Ministers.** Place 9 minister cards face up on your Unswayed Ministers pile.
6. **Fill Crown Spaces.** Place 9 crowns on the spaces with VP on your faction board.

### 12.4 Birdsong
- Place one warrior, plus one per warrior icon showing on the Citadels track, in the Burrow.

### 12.5 Daylight
1. **Assembly:** Take up to two actions in any order and number:
   - **Build:** Reveal one card to place a citadel or market in a matching clearing you rule.
   - **Recruit:** Place one warrior in the Burrow.
   - **Move:** Take a move.
   - **Battle:** Initiate a battle.
   - **Dig:** Spend one card to place a tunnel in a matching clearing without a tunnel. Then move up to four warriors from the Burrow to that clearing.
2. **Parliament:** You may take the action of each swayed minister once in any order:
   - **Foremole (Squire):** Reveal any card to place a citadel or market in any clearing you rule (matching or not).
   - **Captain (Squire):** Initiate a battle.
   - **Marshal (Noble):** Take a move.
   - **Brigadier (Noble):** Take up to two moves or initiate up to two battles.
   - **Banker (Noble):** Spend any number of cards of the same suit to score VP in equal number.
   - **Mayor (Lord):** Take the action of any swayed noble or squire.
   - **Duchess of Mud (Lord):** Score 2 VP if all three tunnels are on the map.
   - **Baron of Dirt (Lord):** Score 1 VP per market on the map.
   - **Earl of Stone (Lord):** Score 1 VP per citadel on the map.
3. **Sway:** You may sway one minister:
   - Choose a minister in your Unswayed Ministers pile. You must have a crown on your board matching that minister's rank.
   - Reveal cards equal to the minister's rank requirement (squire=2, noble=3, lord=4). For each card you reveal, you must have at least one Duchy piece in a clearing matching the card's suit. Each such clearing allows you to reveal one matching card.
   - Take the minister card, place it above your board. Take a crown of that rank from your board and place it on the minister card. Score the VP on the uncovered space.

### 12.6 Evening
1. **Discard and Return Revealed Cards:** Discard any bird cards revealed this turn. Return all other revealed cards to your hand.
2. **Craft:** Activate citadels and markets (identical for crafting).
3. **Draw and Discard:** Draw one card, plus one per draw icon showing. Discard down to five.

## 13. Corvid Conspiracy (Setup Order: H, Reach: 3)

### 13.1 Overview
The Corvids score VP each time they flip a plot token face up. The more revealed plots on the map, the more they score per flip. Misdirection is critical — if their plans are too obvious, they risk exposure.

### 13.2 Faction Rules and Abilities
- **Crafting (13.2.1):** The Corvids craft during Birdsong by activating plot tokens (whether face up or down).
- **Plot Tokens (13.2.2):** The Corvids have eight plot tokens, two of each type.
  - While in supply, plot tokens are face down (feather side). While on the map, they can be face up or face down. The Corvids can inspect any facedown plot token at any time.
  - **Placement Limits:** Each clearing can hold only one plot token.
- **Nimble (13.2.3):** The Corvids can move regardless of who rules their origin or destination clearing.
- **Exposure (13.2.4):** Anytime on their turn, but before drawing cards in their Evening, an enemy player with faction pieces in a clearing with a facedown plot token may show the Corvids a matching card to guess the type. If incorrect, the Corvids say "no" and the enemy gives that card to the Corvids. If correct, the enemy removes the plot token (scoring a VP) and ignores its effect.
- **Embedded Agents (13.2.5):** As defender in battle, if the Corvids have a facedown plot token (even defenseless) in the clearing of battle, they deal an extra hit.

### 13.3 Faction Setup
1. **Gather Warriors and Plots.** Form supplies of 15 warriors and 8 plot tokens face down.
2. **Scatter.** Place 1 warrior in any clearing of each suit (total of 3).

### 13.4 Birdsong
1. **Craft:** You may activate plot tokens to craft cards from your hand.
2. **Flip Plots:** Any number of times, flip a plot token face up in a clearing with any Corvid warriors. Score 1 VP per face-up plot token on the map (including the newly flipped one). Then resolve its flip effect if it is a bomb or extortion.
3. **Recruit:** Once per turn, you may spend any card to place one warrior in each matching clearing. If you spend a bird, choose one suit.

### 13.5 Daylight
You may take up to three actions in any order and number:
- **Move (13.5.1):** Take a move.
- **Plot (13.5.2):** Remove a Corvid warrior, plus another Corvid warrior per plot you have placed this turn, from a clearing with no plot tokens to place a facedown plot token there.
- **Battle (13.5.3):** Initiate a battle.
- **Trick (13.5.4):** Swap two plot tokens on the map. Both must be face up or face down.

### 13.6 Evening
1. **Exert:** You may take one Daylight action if you choose not to draw cards during Evening.
2. **Draw:** Draw one card plus one per face-up extortion token on the map. Discard down to five.

### 13.7 Plot Tokens Reference
- **Bomb:** When flipped, remove all enemy pieces in its clearing, then remove the bomb token.
- **Snare:** While face up, enemy pieces cannot be placed in or moved from its clearing.
- **Extortion:** When flipped, take a random card from each enemy player with faction pieces in its clearing. While face up, draw another card in Evening.
- **Raid:** Whenever a raid token is removed (face up or down), place one warrior in each adjacent clearing. Ignore this effect if removed by Exposure.

## 14. Lord of the Hundreds (Setup Order: I, Reach: 9)

### 14.1 Overview
The Hundreds score during Evening based on how many clearings they oppress — clearings they rule that have a Hundreds piece and no enemy pieces at all. Their army is led by a warlord, a warrior-demagogue whose shifting mood provides temporary abilities. By gaining items for their Hoard, the Hundreds grow in Command and Prowess. They can incite mobs to raze enemy buildings and tokens.

### 14.2 Faction Rules and Abilities
- **Crafting (14.2.1):** The Hundreds craft during Daylight by activating strongholds.
- **The Warlord (14.2.2):** The warlord is a warrior that cannot be removed outside of battle, moved outside of the Hundreds' turn, or placed in ways other than setup and the Anoint action.
- **Contempt for Trade (14.2.3):** Whenever the Hundreds craft an item, they may take the item but score none of the listed VP, OR may remove the item permanently to score the listed VP. They cannot get both. (They can still score extra crafting points from Master Engravers, Legendary Forge, etc.)
- **The Hoard (14.2.4):** Instead of a Crafted Items box, the Hundreds have the Hoard with two tracks:
  - **Command Track:** Holds Boots, Bags, and Coins.
  - **Prowess Track:** Holds Hammers, Tea, Swords, and Crossbows.
  - **Gaining Items:** Items go in the leftmost empty matching space. If no empty space, must permanently remove the gained item or an item on that track, scoring 1 VP.
  - **Command and Prowess values:** 0 items = 1, 1-2 items = 2, 3 items = 3, 4 items = 4.
- **Looters (14.2.5):** At the start of battle as the attacker, the Hundreds may declare they want to loot the defender (if the defender has items in their Crafted Items box). Cannot loot the Vagabond. If looting, the Hundreds deal no rolled hits (defender still deals rolled hits, and the Hundreds can still deal extra hits). At the end of battle, if the Hundreds rule the clearing, they take one item from the defender's Crafted Items box.

### 14.3 Faction Setup
1. **Gather Pieces.** Form supplies of 20 warriors, 1 warlord, and 6 strongholds.
2. **Garrison.** Place warlord, four warriors, and one stronghold in a corner clearing not used by another player, preferably diagonally opposite.
3. **Place Items.** Place the four "R" items randomly under the ruins (unless already done).
4. **Get Stubborn.** Place your Stubborn mood card on your Mood Card slot.

### 14.4 Birdsong
1. **Raze:** In each clearing with a mob token, remove all enemy buildings and tokens. Take one item from the ruin there (if any), and remove the ruin if you took its last item. After resolving all mobs, roll the mob die once and place a mob token in a matching clearing that has no mob token but is adjacent to a clearing with a mob token. If no such clearing exists, do not place a mob.
2. **Recruit:** Place warriors equal to your Prowess into the clearing with your warlord. Then place one warrior at each stronghold.
3. **Anoint:** If the warlord is not on the map, replace any Hundreds warrior on the map with the warlord. If you cannot, place the warlord in any clearing.
4. **Choose Mood:** You must replace your current mood card with a different mood card that does not show an item that is in your Hoard. If you are Lavish and cannot choose a new mood, you remain Lavish.

### 14.5 Daylight
1. **Craft:** Activate strongholds to craft cards.
2. **Command the Hundreds:** Take the following actions, in any order, a number of times up to your Command:
   - **Move:** Take a move.
   - **Battle:** Initiate a battle.
   - **Build:** Spend a card to place a stronghold in a matching clearing you rule.
3. **Advance the Warlord:** Take this action a number of times up to your Prowess. You may move the warlord with any Hundreds warriors, and then you may battle in the warlord's clearing.

### 14.6 Evening
1. **Incite:** Any number of times, spend a card to place a mob token in a matching clearing that has no mob token but has a Hundreds warrior (including your warlord).
2. **Oppress:** Score VP based on clearings you rule that have a Hundreds piece and no enemy pieces: 1-2 clearings = 1 VP, 3-4 = 2 VP, 5 = 3 VP, 6+ = 4 VP.
3. **Draw and Discard:** Draw one card. Discard down to five.

### 14.7 Moods
- **Bitter (Hammer):** In battle in your warlord's clearing, before the roll you may remove any number of mob tokens from your warlord's clearing and adjacent clearings. Place warriors in your warlord's clearing equal to the number of mobs removed.
- **Grandiose (Tea):** This turn, perform Advance the Warlord before Command the Hundreds (reversed order).
- **Jubilant (Boot):** Whenever you Incite in your warlord's clearing, after placing the mob, you may roll the mob die and place a mob up to four times (each in a matching clearing adjacent to any mob, with no existing mob).
- **Lavish (No Item):** At end of Birdsong, may remove any number of items from your Hoard permanently. For each item removed, place two warriors into your warlord's clearing. Then shift items to fill tracks from left to right.
- **Relentless (Bag):** Whenever you Advance and both move and battle, you may then either move your warlord with warriors or battle in your warlord's clearing.
- **Rowdy (Coins):** In Evening, draw one more card. If your warlord's clearing has three or more enemy pieces (even from different enemies), draw two more cards instead.
- **Stubborn (Crossbow):** In battle in your warlord's clearing, ignore the first hit you take. Does not combine with other abilities that let you ignore the first hit.
- **Wrathful (Sword):** As attacker in battle in your warlord's clearing, deal an extra hit.

## 15. Keepers in Iron (Setup Order: J, Reach: 8)

### 15.1 Overview
The Keepers are devout knights who have returned to recover relics lost in past conflicts. They must delve relics from forests, move them to waystations, and recover them to score VP equal to the relic's value. They also score 2 VP for completing a set of all three relic types. Their Retinue of Woodland locals lets them take more actions, but cards may be discarded from it when delving or recovering high-value relics.

### 15.2 Faction Rules and Abilities
- **Relics (15.2.1):** Twelve relic tokens of three types — figures, tablets, and jewelry. Each type has four tokens with values of 1, 2, 3, and 3 (value shown on the back).
- **Waystations (15.2.2):** Three waystations, each showing one relic type on front and another on back.
- **Crafting (15.2.3):** The Keepers craft during Daylight by activating waystations (of any type).
- **Devout Knights (15.2.4):** In battle, if the clearing has at least one relic and at least one Keeper warrior, the Keepers ignore the first hit they take. If ambushed, ignore one hit from the ambush but do not ignore further hits. When moving, Keepers may move one relic with each Keeper warrior moved.
- **Prized Trophies (15.2.5):** Whenever an enemy removes a relic, they place it in any forest face up and score an extra VP (total of two VP for removing it).

### 15.3 Faction Setup
1. **Place Starting Relics.** Collect all twelve relics and shuffle face down. Place one randomly in each forest.
2. **Gather Warriors.** Form a supply of 15 warriors.
3. **Place Warriors.** Place four warriors in a corner clearing not used by another player (preferably diagonally opposite). Place four more warriors in an adjacent edge clearing.
4. **Place Remaining Relics.** Place remaining relics randomly, as evenly as possible, among forests not adjacent to clearings with your warriors.
5. **Tuck Faithful Retainers.** Tuck one Faithful Retainer card into each Retinue slot of your faction board (three total).
6. **Collect Waystations.** Place your three waystations on matching Waystations spaces on your board.

### 15.4 Birdsong
1. **Encamp:** Once per clearing, you may replace a Keeper warrior with a waystation (placed face up or face down). If you have no warriors or waystations on the map at all, instead place one waystation and one warrior in any edge clearing.
2. **Decamp:** Once per clearing, you may replace a waystation with a Keeper warrior.
3. **Recruit:** Any number of times, spend a card to place two warriors at a matching waystation.

### 15.5 Daylight
1. **Craft:** Activate waystations (of any type) to craft cards.
2. **Act with Retinue:** Starting with the leftmost column of your Retinue and moving right, you may take the column's action for each card in the column (in any order):
   - **Move (left slot):** Take a move from a clearing whose suit matches the card suit.
   - **Battle then Delve (middle slot):** Choose a matching clearing. You must battle there if any enemy pieces are present. Then, if you rule the clearing and it has at least one Keeper warrior, you may delve:
     - Move any relic from an adjacent forest into the clearing. Flip it to show its value.
     - Count clearings you rule adjacent to the forest from which you delved. If you rule fewer than the relic's value, you must discard the Retinue card used.
   - **Move or Recover (right slot):** Choose a matching clearing. Take a move from it, or recover relics:
     - **Take Relic:** Take a relic there of the same type as a waystation there. Place it in your leftmost empty Relics space of that type.
     - **Score Points:** Score VP equal to the relic's value. Score 2 additional VP if you filled a Relics column.
     - **Check for Discard:** Count clearings you rule whose suit matches the clearing from which you're recovering. If you rule fewer than the relic's value, you must discard the Retinue card and end this Recover action. Otherwise, you may take another relic or end.

### 15.6 Evening
1. **Live Off the Land:** You must remove one Keeper warrior from each clearing that has four or more Keeper warriors.
2. **Gather Retinue:** Add any number of cards from your hand to any Retinue slots, OR shift one card in your Retinue to a different slot. Your Retinue cannot hold more than ten cards.
3. **Draw and Discard:** Draw one card, plus one per uncovered draw bonus (equal to waystations on the map). Discard down to five.

## Appendix A: Advanced Setup

### A.1 Choose and Set Up Map
Choose a map as a group. If using a variant map, follow its setup modifications.

### A.2 Choose Deck
You may replace the entire standard deck with the Exiles and Partisans deck.

### A.3 Set Up Bots
You may choose to play with bots (described in the Law of Rootbotics).

### A.4 Seat Players
Determine seating order and first player randomly.

### A.5 Set Up Landmarks
You may choose to use one or two landmarks. Landmarks cannot be battled, moved, or removed unless noted.

### A.6 Set Up Hirelings
You may choose to use exactly three hirelings. See Hirelings section below.

### A.7 Draw Five Cards
If playing with two players, remove all four dominance cards. Shuffle the deck. Each player draws five cards.

### A.8 Set Up Factions
Use Standard Setup or the faction setup cards from the Marauder Expansion:
- Deal one militant faction setup card to the pool, then shuffle remaining militant cards with all insurgent cards and deal one per player.
- Starting with the last player in turn order (going counterclockwise), each player chooses one faction and sets up immediately.
- **Two Players:** Remove all insurgent faction setup cards before dealing (may keep them if playing with hirelings).
- **Homelands:** Players cannot choose homelands already chosen by enemies or where they cannot place all setup pieces. Try to set up as far away from enemies as possible.

### A.9 Place Score Markers
Place on "0" on the score track.

### A.10 Choose Starting Hands
Each player chooses three cards to keep from their five and puts the other two face down on the deck. Shuffle the deck when finished.

## Appendix C: Variant Maps

### Winter Map
- **Raging River:** The river divides forests (as printed paths do).

### Lake Map
- Place the Ferry in the corner clearing that is also a coastal clearing.
- **The Lake:** The lake is in the center. Treated as rivers linking each coastal clearing to each other. The lake divides forests.
- **Coastal Clearings:** Clearings touching the lake (not separated by a forest).
- **Coastal Forests:** Forests touching the lake. Each is adjacent to its two neighboring coastal forests (separated by one coastal clearing).
- **The Ferry:** Once per turn, pieces moving from the coastal clearing with the Ferry can move on the lake to any other coastal clearing, ignoring adjacency, and moving the Ferry along. You still must rule origin or destination. After this, the moving player draws one card. The Ferry cannot be battled or removed.

### Mountain Map
- Place 6 closed path markers on the paths of darker color. Place the Tower in the central clearing.
- **Closed Paths:** Clearings linked by a closed path are not adjacent. Closed paths enclose and divide forests as paths do.
- **Removing Closed Paths:** Once per turn in Daylight, a player can spend a card to remove a closed path marker permanently and score 1 VP. Must have any faction pieces in either linked clearing.
- **The Pass and Tower:** The clearing with the Tower is the Pass. At the end of a player's Evening, if they rule the Pass, they score 1 VP.
- **Forests:** All areas enclosed by paths and clearings are forests, regardless of tree art.

## Appendix E: Hirelings

### E.1 Gaining and Losing Hirelings
- **Gaining from Supply:** When your score marker enters a space with a hireling marker, take the marker. At the end of your turn, take any hireling card from the supply, roll for control, and flip over the hireling marker.
- **Rolling for Control:** Roll the control die and place control markers equal to the die roll. Count only gold pips if you have the most VP (or are tied for most). Count all pips otherwise.
- **Gaining from Another Player:** At the end of your turn, remove one control marker from each hireling you gained on previous turns. Give each hireling with no control markers to any other player, who rolls for control immediately.

### E.2 Abilities and Actions
- Hirelings have various actions and abilities marked by icons.
- Actions marked "Controller:" are gained by the hireling's controller.

### E.3 Rules
- **Rule:** A hireling's controller treats hireling pieces as their own for rule only. Uncontrolled hirelings can rule clearings as if they were players.
- **Enemy Status:** A hireling is an enemy to players other than its controller.
- **Separate Pieces:** Hireling pieces are not the controller's faction pieces.
- **Separate Actions:** A hireling's Move or Battle actions can only move or battle with the hireling's pieces.
- **Separate Effects:** Hirelings cannot use their controller's abilities, crafted persistent effects, or ambush cards.
- **No Scoring from Removal:** A hireling's controller does not score VP if their hireling removes an enemy piece. Players can still score by removing hireling buildings and tokens, even when defending.
- **Outrage Interaction:** Moving hireling warriors into a sympathetic clearing or removing Alliance sympathy using a hireling does trigger Outrage.

### Hireling Setup (without Advanced Setup)
1. Collect 12 control markers and the control die. Place hireling markers on the "4," "8," and "12" score track spaces.
2. Shuffle hireling cards, deal three, collect their pieces. Return rest to box.
3. Demote based on player count: 2 players = three Promoted, 3 = two Promoted + one Demoted, 4 = one Promoted + two Demoted, 5+ = three Demoted.
4. Starting with last player in turn order (counterclockwise), each player sets up one hireling.

## Appendix F: Landmarks

### General Rules
- Landmarks cannot be battled, moved, or removed unless noted explicitly by a landmark.
- Landmarks are not owned by any faction and are not enemy pieces.

## Appendix G: Glossary (Key Terms)

- **Adjacent:** A clearing is adjacent to all clearings linked by a path. A forest is adjacent to clearings touching it without crossing a path, and to forests separated by only one path.
- **Building:** A square cardboard piece owned by its faction.
- **Cannot:** Absolute. Cannot be overridden unless explicitly instructed.
- **Discard/Spend:** Place the card in the shared discard pile. If it is a dominance card, place it near the map instead. "Spend" is used in contexts with choice but is equivalent to "discard."
- **Draw:** Take the top card from the shared deck.
- **Enemy:** Any other player not in a coalition with you, or a hireling in the supply or held by an enemy player.
- **Enemy Piece:** A faction piece of an enemy player or a hireling piece of an enemy hireling, except while you're treating it as your own for rule. Items cannot be enemy pieces.
- **Faction Piece:** All warriors, pawns, buildings, and tokens listed on the back of a faction board. Items are not faction pieces.
- **Force:** Resolve effects exactly as if that player were choosing to do it.
- **Forest:** An area on the map enclosed by paths and clearings.
- **Item:** A square cardboard piece showing an item (Boot, Sword, Coins, etc.) not owned by any faction.
- **Map Edge:** The single closed loop of clearings and paths defining the map boundary.
- **Path:** A white link between two clearings.
- **Pawn:** A wooden figure owned by its faction.
- **Place:** Take the piece from the prompted source and put it in the prompted destination. Default source is supply or leftmost piece from a track.
- **Play Area:** The area around your faction board. Cards there can only be spent, discarded, or used for other purposes if explicitly instructed.
- **Remove:** Take piece from prompted source and return to prompted destination. Default: return to owner's supply or rightmost empty track space. Items are removed permanently.
- **Replace:** Shorthand for remove then place. Must complete all steps.
- **Reveal:** Place the prompted card face up in your play area (usually from hand).
- **River:** A blue link between two clearings.
- **Rule:** See section 2.5.
- **Slot:** White box in a clearing.
- **Supply:** Collection of pieces not on a faction board or the map.
- **Swap:** Switch locations of two pieces. Ignores move and place restrictions (e.g., snare, keep).
- **Token:** A circular cardboard piece owned by its faction.
- **Treat:** The prompted thing takes on properties of the second thing. While you treat enemy pieces as your own for rule, they are not enemy pieces but remain faction pieces of the enemy player.
- **Warrior:** A wooden figure owned by its faction. The Vagabond pawn is not a warrior.

## Common Rules Interactions and FAQ

### Battle Edge Cases
- **Ambush hits are not limited** by the number of warriors in the clearing. You can play an ambush card even if you are defenseless.
- **Defenseless extra hit** only applies when the defender has no warriors. This is checked in Step 3, after the roll. Ambush hits may remove attacking warriors before the roll, potentially ending the battle early.
- **Extra hits from effects** (Brutal Tactics, Commander, Embedded Agents, etc.) are never limited by warrior count.
- **Armorers** (persistent card): In battle, you may discard Armorers to ignore all rolled hits taken. This does not affect extra hits or ambush hits.
- **Sappers** (Alliance supporter): The Alliance may spend a bird supporter to deal an extra hit when defending in battle.
- **Field Hospitals** triggers whenever Marquise warriors are removed — even outside of battle. You must spend a card matching the clearing from which warriors were removed to place them at the keep.

### Movement Edge Cases
- You must move at least one warrior when taking a move (zero-warrior moves are not allowed).
- The Vagabond and Corvids are Nimble — they ignore the rule requirement for movement.
- The Riverfolk are Swimmers — they treat rivers as paths and ignore rule when moving along rivers.

### Crafting Edge Cases
- Each crafting piece can only be activated once per turn.
- If a card requires an item that is not in the supply on the map, the card cannot be crafted.
- The Vagabond's Hammers all match the suit of his current clearing for crafting.
- The Riverfolk craft by committing funds to Trade Posts tracks, not by activating crafting pieces.

### Dominance Cards
- You need at least 10 VP to activate a dominance card.
- Once activated, you can never score VP again and cannot replace the dominance card.
- Dominance victory is checked at the start of your Birdsong.
- In two-player games, remove all four dominance cards from the deck.
- The Vagabond cannot activate dominance cards normally; instead, he forms coalitions (in 4+ player games only).

### Rule (Control) Clarifications
- Only warriors and buildings count toward rule. Tokens and pawns do NOT count.
- Ties mean no one rules (except Eyrie with Lords of the Forest, and Lizard Cult with Pilgrims).
- The Lizard Cult's Pilgrims overrides the Eyrie's Lords of the Forest.

### Card and Deck Rules
- Bird cards are wild and can be used as any suit.
- When you must discard or give a non-bird card, you must treat birds as the prompted suit.
- You cannot substitute a non-bird card when a bird card is specifically required.
- When the deck is empty, immediately shuffle the discard pile to form a new deck.
- Hand limit is 5 cards (checked at end of turn during Evening).

### Scoring Clarifications
- Removing enemy buildings and tokens scores 1 VP each for the remover — this applies in battle, revolts, bombs, and any other removal.
- Removing enemy warriors does NOT score VP (except for Vagabond Infamy scoring against Hostile factions).
- Crafting items normally scores the VP listed on the card (exceptions: Eyrie's Disdain for Trade scores only 1 VP, Hundreds' Contempt for Trade gives a choice between item or VP).

### Setup for Different Player Counts
- **2 players:** Remove all four dominance cards. Use factions with combined Reach of 17+. Consider using hirelings. Remove insurgent faction setup cards in Advanced Setup (optional if using hirelings).
- **3 players:** Combined Reach of 18+. If no Vagabond, skip ruin item placement.
- **4 players:** Combined Reach of 21+. Standard four-faction game (Marquise, Eyrie, Alliance, Vagabond) works well.
- **5+ players:** Combined Reach of 25+/28+. Must use Advanced Setup if more than one faction requires a corner clearing.

### Errata and Updates (3rd Printing)
- Faction setup for Eyrie, Lizard Cult, Underground Duchy, and Keepers in Iron has been updated for flexibility: their starting corner clearing should be one "not the starting corner clearing of another player and, if possible, diagonally opposite from a starting corner clearing." This replaces earlier rules that were more rigid about starting positions.
- If the Eyrie must choose a new leader but none are face up (all have been used), flip them all face up (A New Clutch rule).
- The Marquise's starting buildings (sawmill, workshop, recruiter) may be placed among the keep clearing and any adjacent clearings, in any combination.
