# Innovation (4th Edition) — Rules Reference

## Overview

Innovation is a civilization-building card game for 2–4 players by Carl Chudyk. You advance through 11 ages of history (Prehistory through Prudence) by playing cards that represent technologies and ideas. Every card is unique and has its own special ability (called a "dogma effect").

**Key idea:** Cards serve multiple purposes — they can be innovations on your board (giving you abilities), points in your score pile, or achievements you've claimed. Managing this tension is the heart of the game.

**How you win:** Claim enough achievements (6 in a 2-player game, minus 1 for each additional player), or have the highest score when someone tries to draw beyond Age 11, or win through a card effect.

---

## Key Concepts

### The Five Colours
Every card is one of five colours: **Red, Yellow, Green, Blue, or Purple**. Your board has one stack per colour, and only the top card of each colour can be activated.

### Icons
Each card has 4 icon slots (3 icons + 1 hex image that blocks a slot). There are 6 icon types in the game. Visible icons on your board determine whether you can share in or defend against other players' dogma effects. More visible icons = stronger position.

### Value / Age
Each card has a value matching its age (1–11). Higher value = more powerful but harder to access. Your draw deck is determined by your highest top card's value.

### Score Pile
Cards placed in your score pile are worth points equal to their value. Your total score is the sum of all cards in your score pile. Points are not spent when achieving — they're just a threshold.

## Setup

1. Split all cards into 11 age decks, shuffle each
2. Take 1 random card from each Age 1–10 deck, place face-down as **Standard Achievements** (don't look at them)
3. Place the 5 **Special Achievements** nearby
4. Place the **Junk** info card nearby
5. Each player takes a **Reference Card**
6. Each player draws 2 cards from Age 1, simultaneously **melds** one (places face-up as their board) and keeps the other as their hand
7. The player whose melded card title comes **first alphabetically** is first player

**First turn exception:** The first player (or first two in a 4+ player game) takes only 1 action instead of 2 on their initial turn.

---

## Your Turn

Take **2 actions** (you can repeat the same type). Choose from:

### 1. Draw
Take a card into your hand from the deck matching your **highest top card's value**. If that deck is empty, draw from the next higher non-empty deck instead.

**Game-ending rule:** If you would ever draw a card of Age 12 or higher (because all remaining decks are empty), the game ends immediately.

### 2. Meld
Place a card from your hand onto your board. Stack it on top of any existing cards of the same colour. If that colour is splayed, continue the splay.

### 3. Dogma
Activate a **top card** on your board to use its effects. This is the most complex action — see the Dogma section below.

### 4. Achieve
Claim an available Standard Achievement if you meet **both** conditions:
- Your **score** is at least **5× the achievement's age number**
- You have a **top card** of value **equal to or higher** than the achievement's age number

Points are NOT spent. Achievements, once claimed, cannot be taken by anyone else.

---

## Dogma (The Core Action)

Dogma is the heart of Innovation. Here's how it works step by step:

### Step 1: Choose a Card
Pick any **top card** on your board to activate.

### Step 2: Determine Sharing & Demands
Look at the card's **featured icon** (the small icon before each effect — it's whichever icon appears most on the card). Count how many of that icon each player has visible on their board.

- **Eligible to share:** Any player with **at least as many** of the featured icon as you
- **Vulnerable to demands:** Any opponent with **fewer** of the featured icon than you

This is locked in at the start — even if the card moves during the action, keep going.

### Step 3: Execute Effects
Cards can have multiple effects. Execute them **in order**, completing each before moving to the next.

**Demand effects** (dark boxes, start with "I Demand"):
- Read aloud to each **vulnerable** opponent, clockwise from your left
- They must follow the instructions

**Non-demand effects** (light boxes):
- Each **eligible-to-share** opponent executes the effect first (clockwise from your left)
- Then **you** execute it

**The Golden Rule:** Do as much as you can, ignore the rest. If something is impossible, skip it.

**"You may"** = optional. Everything else is **mandatory**, even if it hurts you.

### Step 4: Free Draw
If any opponent **shared** a non-demand effect and it caused something to happen to a card (meld, score, tuck, draw, transfer, etc. — but NOT just revealing), you get **one free Draw action** after the dogma completes.

You do NOT get a free draw from opponents executing demand effects.

## Splaying

Splaying fans out a colour stack to reveal extra icons on covered cards. A colour can be:

- **Unsplayed** (default) — only top card's icons visible
- **Splayed Left** — reveals **1 extra icon** per covered card
- **Splayed Right** — reveals **2 extra icons** per covered card
- **Splayed Up** — reveals **3 extra icons** per covered card
- **Splayed Aslant** — reveals **4 extra icons** per covered card (all icons visible)

Rules:
- You need at least 2 cards of a colour to splay it
- If reduced to 0 or 1 card, the splay is forgotten
- If already splayed, unsplay first before re-splaying in a new direction
- Cannot splay in the same direction it's already splayed

Splaying is crucial — it's how you build up icon counts to defend against demands and share in others' effects.

## Achievements & Winning

### Standard Achievements
10 face-down cards (one from each Age 1–10) set aside during setup. Claim them with the Achieve action when you have enough points and a high enough top card.

### Special Achievements
5 face-up cards with specific conditions (e.g., "have at least four top cards with a DEMAND effect"). Claimed **automatically** when you meet the condition — no action needed, and can even be claimed during another player's turn.

### Chain Achievements
If a self-execute effect triggers another self-execute, draw and achieve an Age 11 card as a Chain Achievement.

### Winning
There are 4 ways to win:

| Method | How |
|---|---|
| **Achievements** | Claim enough: 6 (2p), 5 (3p), 4 (4p). Checked after each action. |
| **Score** | Game ends when someone would draw Age 12+. Highest score wins, then most achievements, then draw. |
| **Card Effect** | Some cards say "you win" if a condition is met. Game ends immediately. |
| **Elimination** | If all other players are eliminated (via card effects), you win. |

## Dogma Effect Keywords Quick Reference

| Keyword | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Draw and X** | Draw from the specified deck, then do X (meld, score, tuck, etc.) to that card |
| **Exchange** | Swap cards between two locations (even if one side is empty) |
| **Junk** | Remove from play entirely (under Junk card). Junking a deck junks all its cards |
| **Lose** | Player is eliminated — all their cards are junked |
| **Return** | Put card face-down at bottom of its deck |
| **Reveal** | Show to all players, then it stays where it was |
| **Score** | Place face-down in your score pile |
| **Self-execute** | Execute all non-demand effects on a card (no sharing, no demands) |
| **Transfer** | Move a card between areas (doesn't count as melding/scoring/etc.) |
| **Tuck** | Place on bottom of matching colour pile (continues splay if possible) |

## Team Rules (Optional)

- Typically 2v2 with teammates across from each other
- Teammates are **never** vulnerable to your demands and are **not** opponents
- Team achievements count together (6 to win)
- If one player wins, the whole team wins
- If game ends by drawing past Age 11, highest **combined team score** wins

## Parley Rules (4+ Players, Non-Team)

In games with 4+ players, you can **Parley** (cost: return a card from your hand) to:
1. Dogma a top card on a **distant** player's board instead of your own
2. Confirm sharing eligibility during a distant player's dogma
3. Become immune to a distant player's demand

Neighbors (adjacent left/right) interact normally without Parley.
